New in 1.8:
- Updates: Embarr checks for a new version once a day and installs it from the menu ("Install Update"), with no download page and no "Open Anyway" step. Check for Updates… is in the right-click menu and the popover's "…" menu; Settings > General turns the daily check off.
- Menu-bar mug: it fills with the battery's charge (green, yellow, red), a flame flickers out of it while the heater is on, and blue lines drift into it while the drink cools toward the goal.
- Off the charger: a minute after the mug leaves the charger, a notification asks whether to turn the heater on or off.

Updating from 1.5 or earlier: the bundle ID changed, so Embarr starts fresh. Pick your mug again, allow Bluetooth again, and re-import programs if you exported them.

Embarr
=========

A small menu-bar app for Ember mugs and cups. It can hold your drink cooler than Ember's own app allows (below
120°F), run programs that step the temperature down as you drink, and turn the heater on, off or pause it.
Needs a Mac with macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer and Bluetooth.


1. Install
----------
- Double-click the zip to unpack it, then drag "Embarr.app" into your Applications folder.
- Open it from Applications. macOS will say it can't check the app for malware: it's an app that Apple
  hasn't reviewed. That's expected. To open it anyway:
    - Close that message (Done, or OK).
    - Within the hour, open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to
      Embarr.
    - Click "Open Anyway" again in the box that appears, and confirm with your password or Touch ID.
  You only do this once per version.
- Allow Bluetooth when it asks. If you clicked Don't Allow, the menu says "Bluetooth not allowed" and has a
  button that opens the right page of System Settings: turn on Embarr there.
- Allow notifications too if you want a "your drink is ready" alert.
- A cup icon appears in the menu bar at the top of the screen. Embarr has no Dock icon.


2. Connect your mug
-------------------
- Your mug needs to have been set up in Ember's own phone app at least once (after any factory reset). Without
  that, the mug won't accept new settings.
- Quit Ember's app on your phone, or turn your phone's Bluetooth off for a moment. A mug only talks to one
  device at a time.
- Wake the mug: pick it up or put it on its coaster.
- Click the cup in the menu bar. Under "Mugs nearby", click Connect next to your mug.
- Not showing up? Put the mug in pairing mode: hold the button on the bottom until the light flashes blue.
- To switch to another mug later: click the small window icon at the top right of the cup's menu, then Mug >
  Forget this mug.


3. Use it
---------
- Click a program to use it; it lights up orange while it runs. Click it again to go back to what was running
  before. (With nothing to go back to, say after a restart, the second click turns the heater off, or on for
  "Just let it cool".) Moving the slider or Turn off ends the program and its orange goes away. Pause turns
  the orange off until the pause ends and the program comes back.
- Right-click a program to rename, edit or delete it. "+ New program" adds one (a hold at one temperature, or a
  glide that steps down over time). "Save as program" keeps the slider's setting.
- The slider holds any temperature. Its colors: blue is cool, amber at 120°F is where the mug holds by itself,
  red is hot.
- Below 120°F the mug can't hold a temperature on its own, so Embarr turns the heater on in short bursts.
  That only works while your Mac is on and near the mug. The drink swings a few degrees around the goal.
- Turn off / Turn on and Pause are near the top. Pause turns the heater off for a while and then goes back to your
  program by itself.
- Rename your mug with the pencil next to its name (it's saved on the mug, so Ember's app shows it too).
- Share programs: open the window (the small window icon), Programs, Share > Export programs. Send the file to
  another Embarr user; they use Share > Import programs. Only the programs go in the file, not your mug.


If something's off, the app keeps a log at ~/Library/Logs/EmberBar.log.
